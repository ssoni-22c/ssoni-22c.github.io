Projects

Severity-dependent proteomic alterations (mild vs. severe status epilepticus)
  • Led a study to examine how behavioral seizure severity shapes the hippocampal proteome from a rat model of acquired epilepsy that was generated by chemoconvulsant-induced prolonged seizures, also known as status epilepticus
  • Conducted proteomic analysis of hippocampal tissue across control, mild-SE, and severe-SE groups investigate molecular effects of subclinical and lower-severity seizures, addressing knowledge gaps in seizure pathology
  • Filtered the processed LC-MS/MS protein data (label-free quantification > 0, spectral counts ≥ 2) and performed K-nearest neighbors imputation, normalization, and multivariate analyses (Volcano plot, PLS-DA, heatmaps, hierarchical clustering) using R-Studio
  • Conducted bioinformatics enrichment with ShinyGO 0.76 for Gene Ontology (Biological Process, Molecular Function, Cellular Component) and KEGG pathway analysis (p < 0.05)
  • Determined statistical significance between groups using Student’s t-test (p < 0.05) using GraphPad Prism
  • Specific results found in the paper
Gold commodity time series analysis
  • Analyzed 60+ years of gold price data (1960-2024) from the World Bank Commodity Price dataset to model the impact of major economic shocks and assess long-run relationships with related assets
  • Diagnosed non-stationarity and trend using ACF/PACF and spectral density analysis; applied log transformation and differencing, then validated model fit using the Ljung-Box test
  • Found that a full-series ARIMA(1,1,2) model failed due to the 1980 gold price shock (Iranian Revolution, Volcker rate hikes), but an ARIMA(1,1,1) model fit well when isolating the 2000-2024 period, and used it to forecast future prices
  • Conducted Johansen cointegration tests to evaluate long-run relationships between gold, silver, and crude oil, finding gold and oil cointegrated across the full period, while gold and silver were cointegrated only after 2000
  • Interpreted results in the context of macroeconomic drivers (inflation, monetary policy, safe-haven demand) and detailed findings in a written report
